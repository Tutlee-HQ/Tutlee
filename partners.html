Why Partner With Tutlee?

Tutlee is more than a marketplace — it's a platform. As a partner, you become part of a global movement connecting students who need help with students who can give it, across universities, borders, and subjects.

Partners are integrated into the Tutlee experience, not placed on the sidelines. From co-branded Study Rings and campus onboarding to curated tutor pipelines and student outcomes data, we ensure your involvement delivers real value — for your students and your institution.

Partnership Opportunities

Partnerships at Tutlee are designed around shared goals. Whether your focus is student access, tutor development, or campus adoption, we work to make your involvement natural and impactful.

Opportunities may include:

  • Co-branded Study Rings for your students
  • Campus-wide tutor onboarding programs
  • Student outcomes and impact data
  • Scholarship-funded tutoring access
  • Joint content and awareness campaigns
