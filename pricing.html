Pricing FAQ

Is creating an account free?
Yes. Creating a Tutlee account is always free — you only pay for a subscription tier if you want the added features, or for the individual sessions you book.
Can I switch plans?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade from Settings at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Do I need Pro to teach on Tutlee?
No — tutoring is available on the Free tier too. Tutor Pro reduces your platform commission and unlocks the Skill Passport and automated scheduling tools.
What's the refund policy?
See our full Refund Policy for details on session cancellations and subscription refunds.
