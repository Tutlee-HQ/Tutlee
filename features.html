Everything you need to learn or teach

Tutlee brings AI-assisted tutor matching, live video sessions, automatic progress tracking, and peer study groups into one platform.

Tutor Match

Matched to your exact weak area — not just the subject

Search a subject, then describe specifically what you're struggling with. Tutlee matches you to tutors verified as strongest in that exact topic, not just broadly "good at Biology."

  • AI-assisted matching based on weak areas, not just subject
  • Tutor strength verified through KYT proficiency testing and pass rates
  • A new match is suggested automatically if your assessment shows no improvement
🎯
🎥
Live Sessions

Video, whiteboard, and chat — all in one session room

Book a session and meet your tutor in a built-in video room with a shared whiteboard for working through problems together, plus in-session chat and file sharing.

  • HD video with mic/camera controls and screen sharing
  • Shared whiteboard for working through problems live
  • Sessions are recorded so you can review them afterward
Assessments

Progress you can actually track, session by session

After every session, Tutlee automatically generates a short assessment based on what was covered. Your score determines whether you move on to the next topic or get matched for another session.

  • Auto-generated based on real session content — not generic quizzes
  • Topic-by-topic progress tracking over time
  • Passing an assessment suggests your next topic automatically
📊
👥
Study Rings

Free, open group sessions hosted by tutors

Join collaborative Study Rings where students prepare for exams together — live video, shared whiteboard, and group chat, open to anyone or invite-only for private groups.

  • Free to join and free to contribute
  • Public or private/invite-only rings
  • Live group video, whiteboard, and discussion
Skill Passport

A verified teaching credential you can actually share

For tutors, Tutlee builds a shareable Skill Passport — a Tutlee-verified record of your session count, learner pass rate, and subject scores that you can send to universities and employers.

  • Updated automatically after every verified session
  • Includes verified credentials, not self-reported claims
  • Downloadable and shareable as a PDF
