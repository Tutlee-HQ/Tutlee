About Tutlee

Tutlee is a peer-to-peer tutoring marketplace connecting students who need academic help with student tutors who can give it — through one-on-one sessions and Study Rings, small structured group sessions built around exam prep and accountability.

Founded and registered in Tallinn, Estonia, Tutlee is built for students everywhere, with an initial focus on West Africa, the UK, continental Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia.
