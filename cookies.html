Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 4 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Tutlee OÜ uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use the Tutlee platform, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, like whether you're signed in, so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

What cookies does Tutlee use?

TypePurposeCan you disable it?
EssentialKeeps you signed in, remembers your learner/tutor mode, supports booking and payment flowsNo — required for Tutlee to function
PreferenceRemembers settings like timezone and display preferencesYes, via browser settings
AnalyticsHelps us understand how the platform is used, so we can improve itYes, via browser settings

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

How can you control cookies?

Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. Since essential cookies are required for core features like signing in and booking sessions, disabling them may prevent parts of Tutlee from working correctly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
